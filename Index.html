Latest Sports News

SRMIST Men’s Football Team Wins Gold

SRMIST Men’s Football Team secured the Gold Medal in the prestigious Founder Chancellor Trophy Football Tournament held at Hindustan University, Chennai. From September 25th to 27th, 2023, SRMIST’s football squad showcased their exceptional teamwork, agility, and sportsmanship throughout the tournament. Their stellar performance culminated in a well-deserved victory, securing the Gold Medal and adding another feather to SRMIST’s cap of sporting achievements.

SRMIST Basketball Team Triumphs Over Vels University

SRMIST’s Basketball Team showcased their dominance by securing a thrilling victory against Vels University in a highly competitive match. The game, held at Vels University Campus, saw SRMIST’s players demonstrating exceptional team coordination, precision shooting, and strong defense.

Led by their captain Prashanth, the team maintained a solid lead throughout the match, finishing with a final score of 88-74. The match's MVP, Madhan, delivered an outstanding performance, scoring 25 points and making crucial assists.

With this victory, the SRMIST Basketball Team has once again proven their strength in collegiate basketball, continuing their legacy of excellence in sports.

SRMIST Kabaddi Team Secures Bronze at Khelo India University Games

The pride of SRM is our Pro Kabaddi Team, which has once again proven its excellence on the national stage. The SRMIST Kabaddi (Men) Team secured the Bronze Medal at the 3rd Khelo India University Games-2022, organized by MYAS AIU & Sports Authority of India.

The tournament, held at Noida, New Delhi, from May 23rd to 26th, 2023, witnessed SRM’s unstoppable performance. Displaying remarkable agility, strength, and strategic gameplay, the team dominated the competition and secured a prestigious spot on the podium.

This victory marks another historic achievement for SRMIST, showcasing the university’s dedication to sports excellence and the relentless spirit of its athletes.
